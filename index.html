Our 4-Step Admission Process

1

Online Registration

Create parent account and fill the bio-data form. Select target class level.

2

Document Submission

Upload birth certificate, report card, and passport photographs online.

3

Entrance Assessment

Attend scheduled physical assessment and interaction with our team.

4

Enrollment

Receive admission letter and complete registration with fee payment.

📅 Important Dates

Admission Window: January - June
Results Release: Within 2 weeks of assessment
New Session: September

Pre-Primary: 3-4 years
Primary 1-2: 5-6 years
